Afterglow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Developer: Tiny Thought

Contact: tinythoughtqa@gmail.com

Afterglow is a private place to write the movie endings you wish had happened, drawn as balloons and polaroids in a sky. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Afterglow Android app ("the app").

The short version

  • Everything you create stays on your phone.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and we never receive your stories, pictures, songs or any other data.
  • You are in control: you can delete your skies and balloons in the app at any time, or remove everything by uninstalling it.

What the app stores on your device

When you use the app, it saves the following only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your stories: the skies, balloons, titles, text, moods, colors, music choices and the strings that link balloons.
  • Your pictures: photos you choose from your gallery or take with the camera, and the colors the app picks from them.
  • Your songs: audio files you choose to add as music. The app keeps a copy inside its own storage.
  • Your settings: simple choices such as whether balloons float or a sky's music is muted.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app. We do not have access to it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera (optional): used only when you choose to take a photo for a balloon. The photo is saved on your phone. You can use the app fully without granting this permission by picking pictures from your gallery instead.
  • Photos, files and music: the app uses Android's system pickers. You choose exactly which picture or audio file to add, and the app only receives the files you select. It does not browse your storage.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, phone number or account.

Sharing and exporting

The app lets you export a sky as a file (to keep a backup or send to a friend) and import such a file. Exporting uses Android's standard share sheet. You decide whether and where the file goes, for example to your files, to email or to a messaging app. Once you share a file, it is handled by the app you chose and is covered by that app's own privacy policy. The exported file can contain your text, pictures and songs, so share it only with people you trust.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it sends nothing to third-party servers.

Backups made by Android

Android may include an app's data in its automatic device backup (for example to your Google account) if you have turned that feature on in your phone's settings. This is a feature of your phone and your Google account, not of the app, and it is governed by Google's policies and your device settings. You can control it in your phone's backup settings.

How long we keep your information, and deleting it

Because the information lives only on your phone, you decide how long it is kept.

  • Delete a balloon or a whole sky inside the app to remove it. Songs that are no longer used by any sky or balloon are cleaned up automatically.
  • Uninstall the app or choose "Clear storage" in Android's app settings to remove everything the app stored on your phone.
  • Files you exported or shared are outside the app. Delete them yourself where you saved or sent them.

Children

Afterglow is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, since the app collects none.

Security

Your content is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. No data leaves your phone through the app. Please protect your phone with a screen lock, since anyone who can open your phone can open the app.

Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on our side. If you have questions about your privacy, or if you believe the app is behaving in a way this policy does not describe, please write to the contact address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if optional cloud saving is ever added), we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above, and the app will ask for your agreement to any new data use before it starts.
